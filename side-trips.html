Ancient Water Towns Near Shanghai

Step back in time at these charming canal towns with Ming and Qing dynasty architecture, traditional bridges, and local delicacies.

Zhujiajiao Water Town

The closest water town to Shanghai (45 min by car). Famous for its 17th-century Fangsheng Bridge, rice dumplings (zhujiajiao zongzi), and scenic canal boat rides through the ancient town.

45 min travel | ¥100-150

Book Zhujiajiao tour →

Zhouzhuang Water Town

Often called "the Venice of the East." Famous for its Shuang Bridge, Wan Three (Wan San) Pig's Trotters, and perfectly preserved canal-side houses dating back to the Ming dynasty.

1.5h travel | ¥120-180

Book Zhouzhuang tour →

Tongli Water Town

A quieter, less crowded alternative to Zhouzhuang. Home to the UNESCO-listed Tuisi Garden and the Pearl Tower. Perfect for travelers seeking an authentic, peaceful water town experience.

1h travel | ¥100-150

Book Tongli tour →

Wuzhen Water Town

One of China's most famous and best-preserved water towns. The East Gate (Dongzha) scenic area features traditional dyeing workshops, ancient opera stages, and enchanting evening canal views.

2h travel | ¥150-200

Book Wuzhen tour →

Suzhou Day Trip

The "Venice of China" is home to 9 UNESCO-listed classical gardens, the ancient Grand Canal, and centuries of silk-making heritage. A must-visit for culture lovers.

  • Humble Administrator's Garden (UNESCO)
  • Tiger Hill & Hanshan Temple
  • Pingjiang Road historic street
  • Suzhou Museum (I.M. Pei design)
  • Jinji Lake & modern SIP district

Trip Details

Duration: Full day (9-10 hours)
Transport: 1.5h by high-speed train
Price: ¥90-120 per person
Includes: Train tickets, guide, 2-3 attractions

Book Suzhou Day Trip →

Hangzhou Day Trip

Capital of Zhejiang province and home to the legendary West Lake, Hangzhou is one of China's most beautiful cities. Marco Polo called it "the finest and most splendid city in the world."

  • West Lake boat ride & pagodas
  • Lingyin Temple & Feilai Feng grottoes
  • Longjing tea village & plantations
  • Nine Creeks & Eighteen Gullies
  • Hefang Street for local snacks

Trip Details

Duration: Full day (10-11 hours)
Transport: 1h by high-speed train
Price: ¥105-150 per person
Includes: Train tickets, guide, 2-3 attractions

Side Trip Tips

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer the best weather. Avoid Chinese national holidays for fewer crowds.

What to Wear

Comfortable walking shoes are essential. Water towns have uneven stone paths. Bring sun protection in summer.

Photography

Early morning is best for crowd-free canal photos. Wuzhen at sunset is magical. Your guide knows the best angles.
