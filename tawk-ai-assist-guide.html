What is AI Assist?

Tawk.to AI Assist is an AI-powered add-on that creates an AI agent to handle customer chats 24/7. It uses your knowledge base, website content, and FAQs to automatically answer visitor questions.

Key Benefits:
  • ✅ Answers chats automatically when you're offline or busy
  • ✅ Trained on YOUR website content and FAQs
  • ✅ Supports Live Chat, Tickets, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS
  • ✅ Seamless hand-off to human agents when needed
  • ✅ Free tier available (100 messages/month)

Step 1: Enable AI Assist

1 Log in to Tawk.to Dashboard → Select your property (shanghaiwondertours.com)
2 Click Add-ons in the left navigation bar
3 Find AI Assist → Click Get Started
4 Switch the AI Assist toggle ON
Note: The free tier gives you 100 AI messages per month. If you need more, paid plans start at $29/month. You can start with the free tier and upgrade later.

Step 2: Create Your AI Agent

After enabling AI Assist, a setup wizard will appear. Follow these steps:

1 Go to Automation → Agents → Click Add your first agent
2 The wizard will ask how you want to create the agent. Choose "Describe your business" option
3 When asked for your website URL, enter: https://www.shanghaiwondertours.com
4 When asked to describe your business, copy and paste the text below

Step 4: FAQ Training Data

The wizard will generate FAQs automatically from your website. Review them, and add these additional ones. You can also paste these directly into the Knowledge → FAQs section later:

Q: What tours do you offer?
A: We offer 6 main tour packages ranging from 3-hour evening tours to 4-day family tours. Our most popular is the Shanghai Classic Full-Day Tour ($89/person). We also offer custom private tours tailored to your interests. Visit our tours page at shanghaiwondertours.com/tours.html for all options.

A: Our tours range from $59/person (Evening Highlights Tour, 3 hours) to $299/person (4-Day Family Disney Tour). Prices vary by tour duration and inclusions. All tours are private with no hidden fees. Group discounts may apply for 4+ travelers.
Q: How do I book a tour?
A: You can book through our website (shanghaiwondertours.com), via WhatsApp at +86 15347723823, or email booking@chinawondertours.com. We recommend WhatsApp for fastest response (usually within minutes). A deposit may be required to confirm your booking.

A: Our guides speak English, Mandarin, Shanghainese, Japanese, French, and German. All our guides are Shanghai natives with deep local knowledge. When booking, let us know your preferred language.
Q: What is included in the tour price?
A: Tour prices typically include: private English-speaking guide, private transportation, entrance tickets to attractions, and some meals (varies by tour). Hotel pickup and drop-off are included for central Shanghai hotels. Specific inclusions are listed on each tour page.
Q: Do you offer hotel pickup?
A: Yes, we provide free hotel pickup and drop-off for hotels in central Shanghai. If your hotel is outside the central area, a small additional fee may apply. Please provide your hotel name and address when booking.
Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the tour start time. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a 50% fee. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable. Weather-related cancellations are fully refundable.
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept PayPal, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfer, WeChat Pay, and Alipay. A deposit is required to confirm booking, with the balance due on the tour day.
Q: How long is each tour?
A: Tour durations vary: Evening Highlights Tour is 3 hours, Food Tour is 4 hours, Classic Full-Day Tour is 8 hours, 2-Day Tour is 2 days, 3-Day Tour is 3 days, and Family Disney Tour is 4 days. Start times are flexible.
Q: Can you arrange airport transfers?
A: Yes, we can arrange airport transfers from Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG) or Shanghai Hongqiao Airport (SHA) as an add-on service. Contact us via WhatsApp for pricing and booking.
Q: What if I have dietary restrictions?
A: We accommodate all dietary restrictions including vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, and food allergies. Please inform us of any dietary needs when booking so we can customize your food tour accordingly.
Q: Is Shanghai safe for tourists?
A: Yes, Shanghai is one of the safest major cities in the world for tourists. Violent crime is extremely rare. Our guides ensure you have a safe and comfortable experience. We are a licensed tour operator with comprehensive insurance.
Q: Do you offer group discounts?
A: Yes, we offer discounts for groups of 4 or more travelers. The discount varies by tour and group size. Contact us via WhatsApp at +86 15347723823 for a custom quote.
Q: What should I bring on the tour?
A: We recommend comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, sunscreen, a hat, and a camera. Bring your passport for attraction entries. We provide bottled water and umbrellas if needed.
Q: Can you help with a visa for China?
A: While we don't process visas directly, we can provide tour confirmation letters to support your visa application. Shanghai also offers 144-hour visa-free transit for eligible nationalities. Contact us for details.
Q: What is the best time to visit Shanghai?
A: The best times to visit Shanghai are Spring (March-May) and Autumn (September-November) when the weather is mild and pleasant. Summer (June-August) is hot and humid, Winter (December-February) is cold but less crowded. We operate tours year-round.
Q: Do you offer tours in other Chinese cities?
A: Our primary focus is Shanghai and nearby water towns like Zhujiajiao. For other Chinese cities (Beijing, Xi'an, Guilin, etc.), we can recommend partner operators. Contact us for multi-city China tour planning.

Step 6: Configure Data Sources

After creating the AI agent, go to Automation → Agents → [Your Agent] → Knowledge and configure these data sources:

Data Source Status Details
Website ✅ Enable URL: https://www.shanghaiwondertours.com
Tawk.to will crawl and index your website automatically
FAQs ✅ Enable The 18 FAQs from Step 4 above
Knowledge Base ✅ Enable Create KB articles in Tawk.to (see below)
Shortcuts ✅ Enable Create shortcuts for common responses (see below)
Documents Optional Upload PDF brochures, tour itineraries, etc.
Text ✅ Enable Custom text specific to this AI agent

2 Try these test messages:
