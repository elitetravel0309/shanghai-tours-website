Tour Overview

See the real Shanghai and its quirks! This unique tour discovers unique perspectives of the city's heritage and daily life as well as some bizarre scenes you won't find on any other tour. The morning starts with a dive into Shanghai's unique park culture and street life, a visit to the Bird and Flower Market, and a journey into the communist past with the Propaganda Poster Museum. In the afternoon you'll head to Qibao Ancient Town to enjoy lunch and soak in 1000 years of history.

This all-inclusive tour is perfect for returning visitors, culture enthusiasts, and anyone who wants to see a side of Shanghai that most tourists miss.

Tour Highlights

🌳

Shanghai Park Culture

Experience authentic local life in a Shanghai park — see locals practicing tai chi, dancing, playing cards, and singing opera.

🌺

Bird and Flower Market

Explore a fascinating market where locals shop for birds, flowers, fish, and small pets — a true glimpse into everyday Shanghai.

🖼️

Propaganda Poster Museum

Visit a hidden gem showcasing China's communist-era propaganda art, offering a unique perspective on the country's modern history.

🏯

Qibao Ancient Town

Explore a 1000-year-old water town with ancient bridges, temples, and streets filled with local snacks and handicrafts.

🍜

Local Food Tasting

Sample authentic Shanghai street food and local delicacies at Qibao's famous food street.

🎭

Chinese Local Life

See real Chinese daily life from hospitals and universities to Walmart — the world's largest Starbucks is also an option!

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup — Park Culture Immersion

Your guide meets you at your hotel. Start with a visit to a local Shanghai park to see morning life — tai chi groups, dancers, singers, and card players. This is real Shanghai at its most authentic.

10:00 AM

Bird and Flower Market

Visit one of Shanghai's famous Bird and Flower Markets. Here you'll find everything from songbirds and goldfish to flowering plants and small pets. It's a colorful and lively experience that locals have enjoyed for generations.

11:00 AM

Propaganda Poster Museum

Discover a hidden gem — the Propaganda Poster Museum, tucked away in an old apartment building. This private collection features original posters from the Mao era, offering a fascinating and thought-provoking look at China's modern history through art.

12:30 PM

Qibao Ancient Town & Lunch

Head to Qibao, a 1000-year-old water town just 30 minutes from downtown Shanghai. Stroll along ancient streets, cross the iconic Qibao Bridge, and visit the Qibao Temple. Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant featuring specialties like Qibao rice wine, fried tofu, and stinky tofu (if you dare!).

3:00 PM

Explore Quirky Shanghai

On the way back, stop at some uniquely Shanghai spots — perhaps the world's largest Starbucks on Nanjing Road, a local supermarket, or a traditional Chinese pharmacy. Your guide can also show you the fascinating Copy Market if you're interested.

5:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide will escort you back to your hotel after a day of unforgettable discoveries.
