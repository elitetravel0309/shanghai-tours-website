How Many Days in Shanghai? The Best Itinerary by Length

How Many Days Do You Need in Shanghai?

For most travelers, 2 to 3 days is the sweet spot for a first visit to Shanghai — enough to see the essential landmarks and taste local life without feeling rushed. Here's how to shape your trip by length.

1 Day in Shanghai — The Highlights

If you have just one day, focus on the city's icons. Start at the Bund along the Huangpu River, visit Yu Garden in the Old City, ride up the Shanghai Tower for the skyline, and end at Nanjing Road. A private guide makes the most of a tight day — our Shanghai Classic Full-Day Tour covers all of this in one smooth route.

2 Days in Shanghai — Add Depth

With two days you can slow down and go deeper. Day one hits the Bund, Yu Garden and the Tower. Day two explores the Former French Concession — Wukang Road, the lanes of Tianzifang — plus a food tour or the Shanghai Museum. Our 2-Day Shanghai City Highlights Tour packs these in comfortably.

3 Days in Shanghai — Add a Day Trip

Three days lets you escape the city. Use days one and two for the highlights and the concessions, then take the third day to the water towns — Zhujiajiao is the most convenient. See the plan in our 3-Day Classic Shanghai Tour, or a water-town version on the 3-Day Shanghai & Water Town.

4-5 Days in Shanghai — Nearby Cities

With four or five days, expand your radius. A Suzhou day trip (classical gardens, 30 min by rail) or a Hangzhou trip (West Lake, tea village) pairs beautifully with Shanghai. Multi-day itineraries like the 4-Day Shanghai & Suzhou or 5-Day Shanghai, Hangzhou & Wuzhen make the logistics effortless.

Short on Time? A Layover

Transiting through Shanghai? Even a few hours can cover the Bund and the skyline. Check eligibility for visa-free transit and browse our Layover Tour for a quick, focused city taste.
