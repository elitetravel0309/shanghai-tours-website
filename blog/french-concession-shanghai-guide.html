French Concession Shanghai: Best Streets, Cafés & What to See

Why Explore the Former French Concession

The Former French Concession is Shanghai's most atmospheric quarter — quiet, leafy streets lined with plane trees, weathered art-deco and colonial villas, boutique cafés and hidden lanes. It's the closest thing the city has to a European district, and a favourite for walkers, photographers and anyone who wants to slow down and feel the romance of old Shanghai.

A Quick Sense of the Area

Carved out in the 19th century as a French settlement, the Concession grew into the city's most elegant residential and cultural quarter. Much of its period architecture survives, and today the area blends heritage villas with trendy shops, galleries and cafés — a living mix of old and new.

A local guide brings the stories behind the facades to life. Our Shanghai Classic Full-Day Tour and the Shanghai Offbeat Charms & Qibao tour both weave through this district.

Best Streets to Walk

Wukang Road

The star of the Concession. Tree-lined and elegant, Wukang Road is lined with villas and apartment blocks from the 1920s-30s, including the famous Normandie Apartments at the crossroads. It's one of Shanghai's most photographed streets.

Huaihai Road

A major shopping and dining boulevard running through the area — bright, busy and stylish, great for window-shopping and people-watching.

Sinan Road

Home to the Sinan Mansions, a cluster of restored garden villas now housing cafés, restaurants and a museum. Peaceful and photogenic.

Tianzifang

A maze of narrow lanes and shikumen houses turned into boutiques, art studios and cafés. It's the best place to pick up handmade crafts and wander without a clear plan.

What to See & Do

  • Admire the architecture: art-deco and colonial villas tucked behind the trees.
  • People-watch at a café: the district is famous for its specialty coffee.
  • Shop at Tianzifang for souvenirs, art and vintage finds.
  • Walk Sinan Mansions for the restored garden-villa lifestyle.
  • Photograph Wukang Road at golden hour.

Best Time to Visit

The Concession is lovely year-round, but autumn — when the plane trees turn golden — is especially beautiful. Mornings are calm; late afternoons bring pleasant light for photos. Evenings are lively with restaurants and bars.

Practical Tips

  • Getting there: metro Line 10 or Line 1 to South Shaanxi Road / Changshu Road stations.
  • Pace: 2-3 hours of easy walking covers the highlights at leisure.
  • Comfort: wear comfortable shoes — it's a walking district.
