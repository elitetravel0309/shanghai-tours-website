Suzhou Day Trip from Shanghai: Gardens, Canals & Silk

Why Take a Suzhou Day Trip

Just 30 minutes from Shanghai by high-speed train, Suzhou is nicknamed the "Venice of the East" for its canals, and is home to some of China's most famous classical gardens — UNESCO World Heritage sites. It's the perfect one-day escape from Shanghai's skyscrapers.

Getting There from Shanghai

  • High-speed rail: ~30 min from Shanghai Hongqiao (SHA) or Shanghai Railway Station. ¥40–60 one way.
  • Taxi/private car: ~1.5–2 hours door-to-door; easiest if you're travelling with luggage or a group.
  • Private tour: door-to-door with a guide handles transport, tickets and route (recommended for a relaxed day).

Must-See Classical Gardens

Humble Administrator's Garden

Suzhou's largest and most famous garden — winding waterways, pavilions and rockeries. Go early or on a weekday to avoid the worst crowds.

Lingering Garden

Elegant and more compact, it excels at framed views and craftsmanship. A great second garden if you have time.

Tiger Hill

The Yunyan Pagoda leans like a miniature Leaning Tower of Pisa surrounded by a scenic hill park — a Suzhou icon worth the climb.

Canals & Old Town

Beyond the gardens, Suzhou's ancient waterways and whitewashed houses along Pingjiang Road are delightful on foot. A short canal-boat ride offers the picture-postcard view of bridges and willow-lined lanes.

Silk & Souvenirs

Suzhou has been China's silk capital for centuries. Visit a silk factory or museum to see sericulture and weaving, and pick up quality silk scarves as souvenirs.
