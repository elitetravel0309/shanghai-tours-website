Top Daytime Attractions

🎢 Shanghai Disneyland Park

China's first Disney theme park features the iconic Enchanted Storybook Castle — the largest Disney castle in the world. Experience TRON Lightcycle Power Run, Pirates of the Caribbean: Battle for the Sunken Treasure, and exclusive attractions designed for Chinese visitors. The park consists of several themed lands with entertainment, dining, and unique experiences.

Regular Day: ¥475 / person
Peak Day: ¥719 / person
Hours: 8:30 AM – 9:30 PM
Location: Pudong, 45 min from downtown

Tip: Book online in advance. Arrive 30 min before opening. Use FastPass for popular rides. Weekday visits have shorter queues.

View our Disneyland Tour Package →

🏙️ Shanghai Tower Observation Deck

The world's second-tallest building at 632 meters. The observation deck on the 118th floor offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the entire city. The high-speed elevator reaches the top in under 60 seconds. On clear days, views extend up to 50 km.

Admission: ¥180 / person
Duration: 1-2 hours

Tip: Book tickets online in advance. Best visited on clear days for maximum visibility.

🎬 Shanghai Film Park

Explore the bustling old Shanghai at this film-making base. Take the tram car tour through 1930s Nanjing Road, old streets & alleyways, the Suzhou Creek Neighborhood, and the Zhejiang Road Steel Bridge. Find Catholic churches, medieval wineries, and European-style buildings. The Costume and Prop Exhibition Halls feature wax figures of famous film stars.

Admission: ¥80 / person
Duration: 3-4 hours

🎪 Shanghai Acrobatic Show

Witness China's most famous acrobats perform incredible feats of strength, agility, and high-flying trapeze acts. The show combines profound history and traditional Chinese martial arts with modern performance. Featuring juggling, balancing, contortion, and intense aerial acts — a must-see cultural experience in Shanghai.

VIP Seats: ¥380 / person
Standard: ¥280 / person
Duration: 90 minutes
Venue: Shanghai Centre Theatre / Circus World

🚢 Huangpu River Night Cruise

The best way to see Shanghai's stunning skyline. This one-hour cruise takes you along the famous Huangpu River, passing the colonial Bund buildings on one side and the futuristic Pudong towers on the other. The lights at night are blazing, decorating different architectural styles along the banks — an unforgettable experience.

Standard Cruise: ¥120 / person
VIP Dinner Cruise: ¥380 / person
Duration: 1 hour
Departure: Shiliupu Wharf (Bund area)
