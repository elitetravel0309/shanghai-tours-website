Shanghai Airport Transfer Service

Private, door-to-door transfers from Pudong (PVG) & Hongqiao (SHA) airports with English-speaking drivers

Why Book a Private Airport Transfer?

The first journey from the airport to your hotel is often the most stressful part of any trip to Shanghai. Public transport can be confusing with language barriers, long queues, and uncertain schedules. A private transfer eliminates all that hassle — your professional driver waits for you at the arrival hall, helps with luggage, and takes you directly to your hotel in a clean, modern vehicle.

Door-to-Door

Your driver meets you at the airport and delivers you to your hotel

24/7 Service

Day or night, we're available whenever your flight arrives

Flight Monitoring

We track your flight and adjust pickup time for delays

All-Inclusive Rates

No hidden fees — tolls, parking, and gratuity included

Shanghai Airports We Serve

Pudong International Airport (PVG)

Shanghai's main international airport, located 30km east of the city center. Transfer time to downtown: 45-70 minutes depending on traffic. We cover all terminals (T1 & T2).

Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)

Shanghai's domestic and regional airport, located 13km west of the city center. Transfer time to downtown: 25-40 minutes. Convenient for domestic flights.
