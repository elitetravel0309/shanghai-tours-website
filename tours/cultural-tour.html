Shanghai Cultural Deep Dive: Temples, Tea & Silk

Tour Overview

Shanghai is not just skyscrapers and shopping — it's a city with deep cultural roots stretching back thousands of years. This tour takes you beneath the surface to explore the spiritual, artistic, and culinary traditions that make Shanghai truly unique.

Visit a sacred Buddhist temple, marvel at ancient treasures in the Shanghai Museum, discover the art of silk-making, participate in a traditional tea ceremony, and try your hand at Chinese calligraphy. This is a journey into the soul of Chinese culture.

Tour Highlights

🙏

Jade Buddha Temple

Visit one of Shanghai's most important Buddhist temples, home to two rare jade Buddha statues brought from Myanmar in 1882.

🏛️

Shanghai Museum

Explore one of China's finest museums with over 120,000 ancient artifacts, including bronze, ceramics, calligraphy, and jade.

🧵

Silk Factory Tour

Learn the fascinating process of silk production — from silkworm to luxurious fabric — with a live weaving demonstration.

🫖

Tea Ceremony

Participate in a guided Gongfu tea ceremony, tasting premium Chinese teas while learning about their history and health benefits.

🖌️

Calligraphy Workshop

Try your hand at Chinese calligraphy with a master artist. Take home your masterpiece as a unique souvenir.

🥬

Vegetarian Temple Lunch

Enjoy a traditional Buddhist vegetarian meal at the temple restaurant, showcasing the incredible variety of plant-based Chinese cuisine.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your cultural guide picks you up and introduces the day's journey into Shanghai's cultural heritage.

9:30 AM

Jade Buddha Temple

Explore this active Buddhist temple. See the magnificent jade Buddha statues, observe monks chanting, and learn about Buddhist traditions in modern China.

11:00 AM

Silk Factory

Visit a working silk factory where you'll see the entire silk-making process — from cocoon to thread to fabric. Try your hand at pulling silk from a cocoon!

12:30 PM

Vegetarian Temple Lunch

Enjoy a delicious Buddhist vegetarian lunch at the temple's restaurant. The dishes are so creative you won't miss the meat!

2:00 PM

Shanghai Museum

Explore the Shanghai Museum's world-class collection. Your guide will lead you through the highlights — ancient bronzes, Ming dynasty ceramics, and exquisite jade carvings.

3:30 PM

Tea Ceremony & Calligraphy

Participate in a Gongfu tea ceremony at a traditional tea house, then try calligraphy with a master artist. Learn to write your name in Chinese characters!

5:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Return to your hotel with your calligraphy masterpiece, a deeper understanding of Chinese culture, and a box of premium tea to take home.
