Tour Overview

Explore Shanghai's vibrant contemporary art scene with an expert guide! This specialized art tour takes you through the city's most exciting creative districts, from the industrial-chic galleries of M50 to the charming narrow lanes of Tianzifang, where traditional architecture meets modern art.

Perfect for art lovers, photographers, and culture enthusiasts, this tour reveals Shanghai's dynamic contemporary art movement. Your private guide will share insights about the artists, galleries, and the fascinating stories behind the works, ensuring a deeply enriching experience.

Tour Highlights

🎨

M50 Mooganshan Road Art District

Discover Shanghai's premier contemporary art hub, home to over 100 galleries and studios housed in renovated industrial buildings along Suzhou Creek.

🏘️

Tianzifang

Wander through the labyrinthine lanes of this former residential area, now transformed into a vibrant arts and crafts enclave with galleries, studios, and boutique shops.

🏗️

1933 Old Millfun

Explore this stunning Art Deco former slaughterhouse, now converted into a creative center with galleries, performance spaces, and unique architectural features.

📚

Duolun Road Cultural Street

Stroll along this historic literary street, home to former residences of famous Chinese writers and artists, with bronze statues and cultural landmarks.

🖼️

Gallery Hopping

Visit multiple contemporary art galleries featuring both established and emerging Chinese artists, with opportunities to meet artists and purchase original works.

☕

Artist Café Experience

Enjoy coffee or tea at a local artist-run café, where you can relax and absorb the creative atmosphere while discussing art with fellow enthusiasts.

Full Itinerary

9:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your art specialist guide will meet you in your hotel lobby. After a brief introduction to Shanghai's contemporary art scene, you'll head to your first destination in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

10:00 AM

M50 Mooganshan Road Art District

Begin at M50, Shanghai's premier contemporary art hub. Your guide will introduce you to the area's history as a textile mill complex, now transformed into over 100 galleries and artist studios. Visit 2-3 galleries featuring both Chinese and international contemporary art.

12:00 PM

1933 Old Millfun

Explore this architectural masterpiece, a 1933 Art Deco former slaughterhouse designed by British architects. Discover its unique concrete structures, spiral ramps, and now thriving creative spaces with galleries and performance venues.

1:00 PM

Lunch at Artist Café

Enjoy lunch at a local artist-run café near the art districts. Your guide will recommend local specialties while you discuss the morning's art discoveries.

2:30 PM

Tianzifang

Wander through the narrow lanes of Tianzifang, where traditional Shikumen architecture houses contemporary art studios, design shops, and galleries. Your guide will point out hidden gems and introduce you to local artists at work.

4:00 PM

Duolun Road Cultural Street

Stroll along this historic literary street, once home to famous Chinese writers like Lu Xun and Mao Dun. Admire the bronze statues, visit former residences turned museums, and discover how this area shaped modern Chinese literature and art.

5:30 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide will escort you back to your hotel, leaving you with a deeper appreciation for Shanghai's thriving contemporary art scene and perhaps a few new artworks for your collection.

Important Information

Walking Level

Moderate to high. Comfortable walking shoes essential. Approximately 12,000-15,000 steps through galleries and narrow lanes.

Accessibility

Some galleries and Tianzifang's narrow lanes may be challenging for wheelchair users. Please inform us in advance for adapted arrangements.

Photography

Photography is generally allowed in galleries and public areas. Some exhibitions may have restrictions — your guide will advise.

Weather

Operates in all weather conditions. Indoor galleries provide shelter. Umbrellas provided if needed.

Art Purchases

Opportunities to purchase original artworks directly from galleries and artists. Your guide can assist with negotiations and shipping.
