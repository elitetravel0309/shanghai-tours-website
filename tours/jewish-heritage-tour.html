Tour Overview

Explore Shanghai's rich Jewish history and heritage on this specialized tour that traces the remarkable story of Jewish refugees who found sanctuary in Shanghai during World War II. From the establishment of the Shanghai Ghetto to the thriving Jewish community that emerged, you'll discover a fascinating chapter of both Chinese and Jewish history.

Your heritage-specialist guide will share personal stories, historical documents, and architectural evidence of this unique period, providing deep insights into the human drama that unfolded in Shanghai's streets.

Tour Highlights

🏛️

Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum

Visit this comprehensive museum dedicated to the Jewish refugees who found shelter in Shanghai during WWII, featuring personal artifacts, photographs, and survivor testimonies.

🏘️

Tilanqiao Historical Area

Explore the historic Tilanqiao area, once known as the "Shanghai Ghetto," where thousands of Jewish refugees lived during the war, preserving their cultural and religious traditions.

🏨

Peace Hotel (Cathay Hotel)

Visit the iconic Art Deco Peace Hotel, once a gathering place for Shanghai's Jewish elite, including the Sassoon and Kadoorie families who played significant roles in Shanghai's development.

🕍

Ohel Moshe Synagogue

Explore this historic synagogue, built in 1927, which served as the spiritual center for Shanghai's Jewish community and now houses the Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum.

📖

Jewish Heritage Stories

Hear compelling personal stories of Jewish refugees who built new lives in Shanghai, contributing to the city's commerce, culture, and society during this remarkable period.

🌍

Cross-Cultural Understanding

Gain insights into the historical relationship between China and the Jewish people, and how Shanghai became a beacon of hope during one of history's darkest periods.

Full Itinerary

9:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your heritage specialist guide will meet you in your hotel lobby. After a brief introduction to Shanghai's Jewish history, you'll head to your first destination in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

10:00 AM

Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum

Begin at this comprehensive museum dedicated to the Jewish refugees who found shelter in Shanghai during WWII. Explore personal artifacts, photographs, documents, and survivor testimonies that bring this history to life.

12:00 PM

Tilanqiao Historical Area Walking Tour

Explore the historic Tilanqiao area, once known as the "Shanghai Ghetto." Walk through the streets where thousands of Jewish refugees lived, visiting preserved buildings and learning about their daily lives during the war.

1:00 PM

Lunch at Local Restaurant

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant while your guide shares more stories about Shanghai's Jewish community and the city's role during WWII.

2:30 PM

Ohel Moshe Synagogue

Visit this historic synagogue, built in 1927, which served as the spiritual center for Shanghai's Jewish community. Learn about its architecture, history, and the vibrant community that gathered here.

3:30 PM

Peace Hotel & Bund History

Visit the iconic Art Deco Peace Hotel (formerly Cathay Hotel), once a gathering place for Shanghai's Jewish elite. Learn about the Sassoon and Kadoorie families who played significant roles in Shanghai's development.

5:00 PM

Reflecting on History

End your tour with time for reflection on this remarkable chapter of history. Your guide will provide final insights into the lasting legacy of Shanghai's Jewish community and its impact on the city today.

5:30 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide will escort you back to your hotel, leaving you with a deeper understanding of this unique chapter in both Chinese and Jewish history.
