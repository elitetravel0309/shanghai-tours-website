Tour Overview

See Shanghai transform into a glittering wonderland on this enchanting evening tour! As the sun sets, the city's famous skyline lights up, creating a magical atmosphere that every visitor must experience. This tour is perfect for those who want to make the most of their evening in Shanghai.

You'll start with a visit to the Bund as the lights come on, then enjoy a spectacular Huangpu River night cruise with stunning views of both the historic Bund and the futuristic Pudong skyline. Your guide will also take you to hidden photo spots and share the stories behind Shanghai's most iconic landmarks.

Tour Highlights

🌃

Bund at Night

Walk along the Bund as the colonial buildings and Pudong skyscrapers light up — one of the world's most spectacular night views.

🚢

Huangpu River Night Cruise

Enjoy a 45-minute cruise along the Huangpu River, with 360° views of Shanghai's dazzling illuminated skyline.

Best Photo Spots

Your guide knows the perfect locations for capturing Shanghai's stunning nightscape — from classic angles to hidden gems.

🏙️

See the iconic Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, and Jin Mao Tower in all their illuminated glory.

🚇

Experience Shanghai's efficient metro system — a fun and easy way to get around the city like a local.

Full Itinerary

6:00 PM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide meets you at your hotel as the evening begins. A quick metro ride takes you to the first stop as the city starts to light up.

6:30 PM

The Bund & Photo Walk

Walk along the Bund waterfront as the colonial buildings and Pudong skyscrapers begin to illuminate. Your guide will point out the best photo spots and share fascinating stories about the buildings' history. Watch as the sky changes colors during sunset.

7:30 PM

Huangpu River Night Cruise

Board your cruise boat for a 45-minute journey along the Huangpu River. From the water, you'll see the full spectacle of Shanghai's illuminated skyline — the historic Bund on one side and the futuristic Pudong skyline on the other. It's a view you'll never forget.

8:30 PM

Lujiazui Skyline Walk

After the cruise, cross to the Pudong side and walk through the Lujiazui financial district, surrounded by the towering illuminated skyscrapers. Your guide will take you to the best spots for photos with the Oriental Pearl Tower and Shanghai Tower.

9:15 PM

Optional: Rooftop Bar Stop

If you'd like, your guide can recommend a nearby rooftop bar for a drink with a view. The skyline from above is absolutely breathtaking at night. (Drinks at your own expense.)

10:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide escorts you back to your hotel, leaving you with memories of Shanghai's unforgettable nightscape.
