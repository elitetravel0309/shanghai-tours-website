Tour Overview

When the sun goes down, Shanghai transforms into a glittering spectacle of neon, lights, and reflections. This evening tour captures the city at its most magical — from the illuminated Bund to the dazzling Pudong skyline, with a relaxing Huangpu River cruise at the heart of the experience.

Perfect for photographers, romantics, and anyone who wants to see Shanghai in its most photogenic form. This short tour is also an ideal complement to a day of sightseeing.

Tour Highlights

🚢

Huangpu River Night Cruise

See the city from the water as both sides of the river compete in a dazzling light display. Drink included.

🏛️

Bund at Night

Walk along the beautifully lit Bund. The colonial buildings glow gold, creating a perfect contrast with the modern Pudong skyline.

🌃

Pudong Skyline View

Capture the best photo of the Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, and Jin Mao Tower lit up against the night sky.

🍸

Optional Rooftop Bar Stop

End the evening with a cocktail at a rooftop bar with uninterrupted views of the skyline (drinks at own cost).

Full Itinerary

6:30 PM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide meets you in the evening as the city lights begin to turn on.

7:00 PM

Bund Evening Stroll

Walk along the Bund as the historical buildings are illuminated. Your guide will point out the best photo spots and share stories of old Shanghai.

7:45 PM

Huangpu River Cruise

Board a cruise boat for a 45-minute journey along the Huangpu River. Watch the Pudong skyline sparkle from the water. A drink is included.

8:45 PM

Lujiazui Photo Stop

Cross to the Pudong side for an up-close view of the skyscrapers and a different perspective looking back at the Bund.

9:30 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Return to your hotel with stunning photos and memories of Shanghai's dazzling nightscape.
