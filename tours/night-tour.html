Tour Overview

When the sun goes down, Shanghai transforms into a glittering spectacle of neon, lights, and reflections. This evening tour captures the city at its most magical — from the illuminated Bund to the dazzling Pudong skyline, with a relaxing Huangpu River cruise at the heart of the experience.

Perfect for photographers, romantics, and anyone who wants to see Shanghai in its most photogenic form. This short tour is also an ideal complement to a day of sightseeing.

Shanghai transforms into a dazzling spectacle after dark. The Pudong skyline lights up with thousands of LED lights, the Huangpu River reflects the city's neon glow, and the historic Bund takes on a romantic golden hue. This evening tour takes you through the most magical side of Shanghai — a city that truly comes alive at night.

Your journey begins with a relaxing Huangpu River cruise, where you'll see the iconic Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, and Jin Mao Tower illuminated against the night sky. Then explore the bustling streets of the Bund area, the French Concession with its tree-lined avenues and historic villas. Your guide will share fascinating stories about Shanghai's transformation from a small fishing village to one of the world's greatest metropolises.

Full Itinerary

6:00 PM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide picks you up from your hotel and gives you an overview of the evening's plan. Drive towards the Bund area as the city lights begin to turn on.

6:30 PM

Huangpu River Night Cruise

Board a comfortable cruise boat and set sail on the Huangpu River. Watch the sunset transform into a dazzling display of lights as both Pudong's futuristic skyline and the Bund's historic architecture are illuminated. The cruise lasts approximately 45 minutes.

7:30 PM

The Bund Promenade Walk

Stroll along the famous Bund promenade, taking in the 26 colonial-era buildings that line the riverfront. Your guide shares the history of each building, from the Customs House to the Peace Hotel.

8:15 PM

Old City & Yu Garden Night Market

Explore the lantern-lit streets of Shanghai's Old City. The area around Yu Garden transforms into a lively night market with stalls selling local snacks, souvenirs, and handicrafts. See the iconic Jiuqu Bridge illuminated at night.

9:00 PM

French Concession Evening Walk

Drive to the charming French Concession area. Take a leisurely walk along tree-lined avenues like Huaihai Road and Wukang Road, passing historic villas, boutique bars, and cozy cafes.

9:45 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide escorts you back to your hotel with a list of recommendations for Shanghai's best nightlife spots and late-night eats.
