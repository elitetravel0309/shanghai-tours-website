Tour Overview

The ultimate combination of urban sophistication and natural wonder. This 6-day tour takes you from the dazzling skyscrapers of Shanghai to the misty peaks of Mount Huangshan (Yellow Mountain), one of China's most famous and beautiful mountains. Explore ancient villages, ride the high-speed train, and experience the best of both worlds — China's most dynamic city and its most celebrated natural landscape.

Tour Highlights

🏙️

Shanghai City Highlights

The Bund, Yu Garden, Shanghai Tower, and the French Concession.

🏔️

Mount Huangshan

One of China's most famous mountains — sea of clouds, ancient pines, and granite peaks.

🏘️

Hongcun Ancient Village

UNESCO World Heritage village with Ming and Qing dynasty architecture.

🚄

High-Speed Train

Shanghai to Huangshan in just 2.5 hours.

Sunrise at Huangshan

Optional sunrise hike to witness the sea of clouds from the summit.

🏛️

Tunxi Old Street

A well-preserved Ming and Qing dynasty street with traditional shops.

Full Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Shanghai

Welcome to Shanghai! Your guide and driver will meet you at the airport.

Day 2

Shanghai Full-Day City Tour

Morning: Visit Yu Garden and the Yuyuan Bazaar.

Afternoon: Walk along The Bund. Ascend Shanghai Tower. Explore the French Concession.

Day 3

High-Speed Train to Huangshan

Ride the 2.5-hour high-speed train to Huangshan. Visit Tunxi Old Street.

Day 4

Huangshan Yellow Mountain

Full Day: Take the cable car up and explore the mountain's stunning scenery. Visit Welcome Pine and Bright Summit Peak. Optional sunset viewing.

Day 5

Hongcun Village

Visit Hongcun Ancient Village, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Explore the Moon Pond and traditional Hui-style houses.

Day 6

Departure

Morning: Free time for souvenir shopping. Transfer to airport or station for departure.
