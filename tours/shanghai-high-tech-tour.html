Tour Overview

Shanghai is a global leader in technology and innovation, and this tour puts you at the forefront of the future. Experience the cutting-edge X-Meta VR full-sensory adventure, where you'll don a haptic vest and battle digital warriors in an immersive world. Watch a drone deliver your coffee at CapitaLand, and enjoy a creative lunch at the iconic No.3 Warehouse — a revitalized industrial space turned culinary destination.

Beyond the tech, you'll explore the timeless beauty of Yu Garden, stroll along Nanjing Road, and take in the iconic skyline along the Bund. This tour is a perfect blend of tomorrow's innovations and yesterday's charm — all in one unforgettable day.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide picks you up and heads to North Sichuan Road, a hub of tech and creativity.

9:30 AM

X-Meta VR Experience

Step into the future with a full-sensory VR adventure. The haptic vest lets you feel every punch, blast, and movement as you battle Terracotta Warriors in stunning digital detail.

12:00 PM

Lunch at No.3 Warehouse

Enjoy a creative dining experience at No.3 Warehouse, a converted industrial space serving innovative cuisine in a trendy setting.

2:00 PM

Drone Coffee & Yu Garden

Watch a drone deliver your coffee at CapitaLand, then explore the serene Yu Garden and the bustling Yuyuan Bazaar.

4:00 PM

Nanjing Road to The Bund

Stroll along Nanjing Road, one of the world's busiest shopping streets, and end at The Bund for iconic skyline views.

5:30 PM

Optional: Huangpu River Ferry

Cross the river on a public ferry for a different perspective of the Pudong skyline. Your guide will escort you back to your hotel afterward.
