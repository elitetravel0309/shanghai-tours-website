Tour Overview

Escape the city and discover the enchanting Zhujiajiao Water Town — often called the "Venice of Shanghai" — on this immersive tour that combines the beauty of ancient canals with a unique hands-on craft experience.

This is more than just a sightseeing trip. In addition to exploring the 1,700-year-old water town with its stone bridges, narrow alleyways, and canals, you'll also participate in a traditional craft workshop. Choose from rice wine making, bamboo weaving, or traditional pastry making — and take home your own handmade creation!

Tour Highlights

🚤

Gondola Ride

Glide along ancient canals in a traditional wooden boat, passing under stone bridges and between historic buildings.

🏯

Zhujiajiao Old Town

Explore narrow alleyways, ancient temples, and the famous Fangsheng Bridge — the largest stone bridge in the water town.

🎨

Hands-On Craft Workshop

Choose from rice wine making, bamboo weaving, or traditional pastry making. A local artisan will guide you step by step.

🍜

Waterfront Lunch

Enjoy a delicious lunch at a local restaurant overlooking the canals, featuring fresh water town specialties.

📸

Kezhi Garden

Visit a hidden Ming Dynasty garden within the water town, with beautiful ponds, pavilions, and ancient rockeries.

🛍️

Local Market

Browse the town's shops and stalls selling local handicrafts, snacks, and souvenirs — perfect for gifts.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide and driver will pick you up at your hotel. Enjoy a comfortable 40-minute drive to Zhujiajiao through the Shanghai countryside.

10:00 AM

Gondola Ride & Water Town Exploration

Start your visit with a traditional gondola ride through the ancient canals. Afterward, explore the charming streets, cross the iconic Fangsheng Bridge, and visit the Kezhi Garden and Zhujiajiao Temple.

12:00 PM

Waterfront Lunch

Enjoy a delicious local lunch at a restaurant overlooking the water. Specialties include river fish, bamboo shoots, and Zhujiajiao rice wine.

1:30 PM

Craft Workshop (Choose One)

Option A — Rice Wine Making: Visit a traditional rice wine workshop where you'll learn the fermentation process and bottle your own batch to take home.
Option B — Bamboo Weaving: Work with a local artisan to weave a small basket or mat from bamboo — a sustainable and beautiful souvenir.
Option C — Traditional Pastry Making: Learn to make Zhujiajiao's famous sweet pastries, then enjoy them fresh from the oven.

3:30 PM

Free Time & Market Visit

Enjoy free time to explore the town at your own pace. Browse the local market for handicrafts, sample street food, or simply relax by the canals.

4:30 PM

Return to Hotel

Your driver will take you back to your hotel, arriving in the late afternoon with your handmade souvenir.

Important Information

Walking Level

Moderate. Comfortable shoes recommended. The water town has stone streets and bridges.

Best Time

Zhujiajiao is beautiful year-round. Weekdays are less crowded than weekends.

Weather

The tour operates in all weather. Bring an umbrella in summer and a coat in winter.
