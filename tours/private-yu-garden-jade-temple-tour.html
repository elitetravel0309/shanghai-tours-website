Tour Overview

Discover Shanghai's most iconic landmarks on this private half-day tour! Visit the Jade Buddha Temple with its exquisite jade sculptures, stroll through the Bund to admire the stunning skyline, explore the serene Yu Garden, and wander the tree-lined streets of the charming French Concession.

This private tour offers flexibility and personalized attention — your guide adapts the itinerary to your interests and pace. With hotel pickup and drop-off included, this is the most convenient way to experience Shanghai's highlights in just half a day.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide and driver will meet you at your Shanghai city hotel (or cruise port). Board your private air-conditioned vehicle and receive a brief orientation about the day's route.

9:30 AM

Jade Buddha Temple

Visit the revered Jade Buddha Temple, home to two exquisite jade Buddha statues brought from Burma. Observe devotees at worship and learn about Buddhist culture and traditions. Note: Temple closes at 4:20 PM — morning visit recommended.

10:30 AM

The Bund

Head to the iconic Bund to admire the stunning Huangpu River skyline. See the Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, and Jin Mao Tower across the river, alongside the colonial-era buildings that tell the story of Shanghai's rich history.

11:30 AM

Yu Garden & Yuyuan Bazaar

Explore the magnificent Yu Garden (Yuyuan), a 400-year-old Ming Dynasty classical garden. Afterward, wander through the bustling Yuyuan Bazaar for shopping, local snacks, and photo opportunities. Note: Closed on Mondays.

1:00 PM

French Concession Walk

Stroll through the beautiful Former French Concession, with its tree-lined avenues, French-style architecture, and charming boutiques. Learn about the 19th-century colonial history and how this area became one of Shanghai's most desirable neighborhoods.

2:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide escorts you back to your hotel. Before leaving, they'll provide recommendations for the rest of your stay and help you plan any additional activities.
