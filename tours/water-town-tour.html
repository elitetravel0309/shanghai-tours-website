Tour Overview

Just 45 minutes from downtown Shanghai lies a different world. Zhujiajiao, known as the "Venice of Shanghai," is a 1,700-year-old water town where ancient stone bridges arch over tranquil canals, traditional houses line narrow alleyways, and life moves at the pace of a gently rowed gondola.

This half-day escape combines the romance of the water town with a traditional tea ceremony led by a local tea master. It's the perfect antidote to Shanghai's urban energy — a peaceful journey into China's rich water town heritage.

Tour Highlights

🚣

Gondola Ride

Glide through ancient waterways in a traditional wooden boat, passing under stone bridges and past centuries-old residences.

🏡

Kezhi Garden

Explore a hidden Ming Dynasty scholar's garden with exquisite rockeries, pavilions, and a tranquil koi pond.

🫖

Traditional Tea Ceremony

Learn about Chinese tea culture from a master and sample premium oolong, green, and pu'er teas in a historic tea house.

🌉

Fangsheng Bridge

Walk across the iconic 5-arched stone bridge, the largest of its kind in Shanghai region, built in 1571.

🍲

Waterfront Lunch

Enjoy a delicious lunch at a family-run restaurant overlooking the canal, featuring local river fish and seasonal vegetables.

🎨

Local Crafts

Browse artisan shops selling hand-painted fans, embroidery, and traditional Chinese paintings along the ancient streets.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide picks you up in a private vehicle and you head west out of the city towards the water town.

9:45 AM

Arrival at Zhujiajiao

Step back in time as you enter the ancient water town. Begin with a stroll along the canals, crossing the iconic Fangsheng Bridge.

10:15 AM

Gondola Ride

Board a traditional wooden boat for a 30-minute ride through the waterways. Your boatman will sing a local folk song as you glide under ancient bridges.

11:00 AM

Kezhi Garden

Explore this beautifully preserved Ming Dynasty garden, once the private retreat of a wealthy scholar. Perfect photo opportunities at every turn.

12:00 PM

Waterfront Lunch

Sit down at a family-run restaurant with canal views. Try local specialties like river shrimp, steamed white fish, and water spinach.

1:30 PM

Tea Ceremony

Visit a traditional tea house for a guided tea ceremony. Learn about the different types of Chinese tea, proper brewing techniques, and the philosophy of tea.

2:30 PM

Free Time & Shopping

Explore the ancient streets at your own pace. Browse for souvenirs — hand-painted fans, local tea, and traditional crafts.

3:30 PM

Return to Shanghai

Relax on the drive back to Shanghai with drop-off at your hotel around 4:00 PM.
