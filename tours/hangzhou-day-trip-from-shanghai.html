Tour Overview

Travel 45 minutes by high-speed train from Shanghai to Hangzhou, one of China's most beautiful cities. Famous for the breathtaking West Lake, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the world-renowned Longjing (Dragon Well) tea, Hangzhou has inspired poets, artists, and emperors for centuries.

Your day includes a scenic cruise on West Lake, a visit to Lingyin Temple — one of China's oldest and most important Buddhist temples — a tour of a Longjing tea plantation where you'll taste the finest green tea, and a stroll through the lively Hefang Street. This is a perfect escape from Shanghai's urban pace into the serene beauty of nature and tradition.

Tour Highlights

🚢

West Lake Cruise

Glide across the tranquil waters of West Lake, surrounded by pagodas, willow trees, and misty hills.

🏛️

Lingyin Temple

🍵

Longjing Tea Plantation

🏮

Hefang Street

🍜

Full Itinerary

7:30 AM

Hotel Pickup & High-Speed Train

Your guide picks you up and escorts you to the train station. Board the high-speed train to Hangzhou — the 45-minute ride flies by.

9:00 AM

West Lake Cruise

Step aboard a traditional boat and cruise across the legendary West Lake. Take in views of Leifeng Pagoda, Su Causeway, and the Three Pools Mirroring the Moon.

11:00 AM

Longjing Tea Plantation

Visit a family-run tea plantation in the hills. Walk through the terraced tea fields, learn about the tea-making process, and enjoy a freshly brewed cup of Longjing tea.

12:30 PM

Local Lunch

Savor authentic Hangzhou cuisine at a local restaurant. Try signature dishes like Dongpo pork, West Lake vinegar fish, and Longjing shrimp.

2:00 PM

Lingyin Temple

Explore the ancient Lingyin Temple, one of the oldest Buddhist temples in China. Marvel at the intricate stone carvings and the serene temple grounds surrounded by towering trees.

4:00 PM

Hefang Street & Return to Shanghai

Stroll through Hefang Street, Hangzhou's most famous pedestrian street, for last-minute shopping and snacks. Then board the high-speed train back to Shanghai.
