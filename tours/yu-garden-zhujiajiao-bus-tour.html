Tour Overview

The Biggest Advantage: This tour is designed to maximize your time, allowing you to experience Shanghai's most famous landmarks and attractions in just one unforgettable day! From the exquisite Ming Dynasty garden to the enchanting canals of Zhujiajiao Water Town, and concluding with stunning Bund night views — this is the ultimate Shanghai day tour.

Enjoy a hassle-free, shopping-free journey with an expert English-speaking guide who shares insider knowledge for a truly refined travel experience. All entrance tickets and a Shanghai-style snack lunch are included.

Tour Highlights

🏯

Yu Garden

A 400-year-old Ming Dynasty garden masterpiece — explore tranquil pavilions, ponds, and exquisite rock formations in the heart of Shanghai.

🏛️

City God Temple Quarter

Step into Old Shanghai's living heritage, with bustling bazaar streets, traditional architecture, and authentic local atmosphere.

🚣

Zhujiajiao Water Town

Known as the "Venice of Shanghai," explore ancient stone bridges, serene canals, and traditional private gardens in this 1,000-year-old gem.

🌆

The Bund Night Views

Conclude with stunning skyline views at Shanghai's iconic Bund, witnessing the "World Architecture Gallery" illuminated at dusk.

🛶

Gondola & Canal Boat Ride

Glide through Zhujiajiao's ancient canals on a traditional wooden gondola, passing centuries-old stone bridges and waterside houses.

🍵

Tea & Local Snacks

Savor authentic Zhujiajiao specialties — zongzi (rice dumplings), braised pork, and a traditional cup of Chinese tea by the canalside.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Meet at Meeting Point

Meet your guide at the Gate of Renaissance Shanghai Yu Garden Hotel. Take Metro Line 10 or 14 to Yuyuan Garden Station, Exit 3. Look for our guide holding a "Mubus: Shanghai City Tour" sign.

9:10 AM

Yu Garden Visit

Visit the exquisite Yu Garden (Yuyuan), a classic example of Chinese imperial garden design. Admire its tranquil pavilions, ponds, and rock formations dating back to the Ming Dynasty (1559).

10:10 AM

Shanghai Old Town & City God Temple

Explore the Chenghuang Temple (City God Temple) Historical Area, immersing yourself in the lively local atmosphere. Wander through traditional streets, visit a tea shop (based on your interest), and soak in the old Shanghai charm.

11:30 AM

Depart for Zhujiajiao

Board the comfortable bus and depart for the enchanting Zhujiajiao Water Town. Enjoy the scenic 1-hour drive through the Shanghai countryside.

12:30 PM

Shanghai-style Snack Lunch

Upon arrival in Zhujiajiao, enjoy a delicious Shanghai-style snack lunch. Sample local specialties that capture the authentic flavors of the region.

1:30 PM

Zhujiajiao Water Town Exploration

Dive into the heart of Zhujiajiao, known as the "Venice of Shanghai." Explore its ancient stone bridges, serene canals lined with Ming and Qing Dynasty buildings, and traditional private gardens. Take a boat ride along the waterways (optional) and capture unforgettable photos.

4:30 PM

Return to Shanghai

Conclude your water town exploration and board the bus for your return journey to Shanghai. Relax and enjoy the ride back to the city.

6:00 PM

The Bund & Tour Conclusion

Arrive at Shanghai's iconic Bund. Admire the "World Architecture Gallery" while our guide shares its fascinating history. This concludes our official tour, after which you may freely explore the Bund and enjoy its stunning night views at your own pace.
