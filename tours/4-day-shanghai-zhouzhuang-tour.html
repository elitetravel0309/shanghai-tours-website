Tour Overview

Discover the enchanting contrast between Shanghai's futuristic skyline and the timeless beauty of Zhouzhuang, known as the 'Venice of the East.' This 4-day tour combines Shanghai's top attractions with a full day exploring Zhouzhuang's intricate network of canals, stone bridges, and Ming Dynasty residences. Experience the best of old and new China in one unforgettable journey.

Tour Highlights

🏙️

Shanghai City Highlights

Visit the Bund, Yu Garden, Shanghai Tower, and the French Concession.

🛶

Zhouzhuang Water Town

China's most famous water town, known as the 'Venice of the East,' with 14 ancient bridges.

🏯

Shen's House & Zhang's House

Explore well-preserved Ming and Qing dynasty residences.

🌉

Twin Bridges of Zhouzhuang

See the iconic Shuangqiao (Twin Bridges), the symbol of Zhouzhuang.

🚣

Gondola Ride

Drift through ancient canals under stone bridges for a magical experience.

🍜

Local Cuisine

Taste authentic Shanghainese and Zhouzhuang specialties.

Full Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Shanghai

Welcome to Shanghai! Your private guide and driver will meet you at the airport and escort you to your hotel.

Day 2

Shanghai City Highlights

Morning: Visit Yu Garden and the Yuyuan Bazaar.

Afternoon: Walk along The Bund. Ascend Shanghai Tower. Explore the French Concession and Tianzifang.

Day 3

Zhouzhuang Water Town Full Day

Morning: Drive 1.5 hours to Zhouzhuang. Explore the Twin Bridges and Shen's House.

Afternoon: Take a gondola ride through the canals. Visit Zhang's House. Enjoy local specialties like Wansan Pork Trotter.

Day 4

Shanghai Museum → Departure

Morning: Visit the Shanghai Museum at People's Square.

Afternoon: Transfer to airport or station for departure.

Important Information

Water Town Experience

Zhouzhuang is the most famous water town in China, with 900 years of history.

Photo Tips

Best photos at the Twin Bridges and from the gondola at golden hour.

Visa-Free Transit

Shanghai offers 240-hour visa-free transit.

Travel Time

Zhouzhuang is approximately 1.5 hours by car from Shanghai.

Local Food

Must-try: Wansan Pork Trotter, river shrimp, and sesame cakes.
