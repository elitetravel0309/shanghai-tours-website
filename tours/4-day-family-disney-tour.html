Tour Overview

Create unforgettable family memories with this perfectly balanced 4-day Shanghai tour! Combining the magic of Shanghai Disneyland with the city's top cultural attractions, this family-friendly itinerary is designed for parents and children to enjoy together. From the ancient Yu Garden and the towering Shanghai Tower to a boat ride through Zhujiajiao Water Town and a full day of Disney adventure — every day brings something new and exciting.

Your private English-speaking guide and driver ensure a stress-free experience, with flexible pacing suitable for families with children of all ages. Let us handle the logistics while you focus on making memories!

Full Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Shanghai

Welcome to Shanghai! Upon arrival at Pudong (PVG) or Hongqiao (SHA) airport/station, your private guide and driver will greet you at the arrival hall and escort you to your hotel in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

After check-in, the rest of the day is yours to relax and recover from your journey. Your guide can recommend nearby family-friendly restaurants or activities to help you settle in.

Day 2

Shanghai City Highlights Tour

Morning: After breakfast, your guide will pick you up from your hotel. Start at Yu Garden, a magnificent Ming Dynasty classical garden built over 460 years ago. Kids will love the intricate rockeries, koi fish ponds, and the famous Nine-Bend Bridge. Then explore the bustling Yuyuan Bazaar where you can taste local snacks like xiaolongbao (soup dumplings) and pan-fried pork buns.

Midday: Visit Xintiandi, a stylish district blending traditional Shikumen architecture with modern shops and cafés. Enjoy lunch at a family-friendly restaurant (included).

Afternoon: Walk along The Bund and take a public ferry across the Huangpu River to Pudong. Then ascend Shanghai Tower — China's tallest building — via the world's fastest elevator to the 118th-floor observation deck for 360° panoramic views.

Optional Evening: Watch the Shanghai Acrobatic Show — a spectacular performance that kids and adults alike will love!

Day 3

Shanghai Disneyland — Full Day

Full Day at Shanghai Disneyland! After breakfast, your driver will take you to the park (approximately 40 minutes from downtown).

Shanghai Disneyland is Asia's largest Disney park, featuring seven themed lands: Mickey Avenue, Gardens of Imagination, Fantasyland, Treasure Cove, Adventure Isle, Tomorrowland, and the newest — Zootopia.

Must-Ride Family Attractions:

  • TRON Lightcycle Power Run — High-speed motorcycle-style coaster
  • Pirates of the Caribbean — Battle for the Sunken Treasure — Immersive high-tech boat ride
  • Peter Pan's Flight — Gentle flying adventure for all ages
  • Soaring Over the Horizon — Breathtaking simulated hang-gliding experience

Tips: Download the Shanghai Disney Resort app for real-time wait times. Stay for the spectacular Ignite the Dream nighttime fireworks and projection show!

Your driver will be available to take you back to your hotel after the park closes.

Day 4

Zhujiajiao Water Town → Departure

Morning: Drive approximately 48 km (30 miles) to Zhujiajiao Water Town, known as the "Venice of Shanghai." This 1,700-year-old ancient town features charming canals, stone bridges, and traditional white-walled buildings.

Explore North Street (Bei Dajie), Fangsheng Bridge (the largest five-arch stone bridge in Shanghai), and the Qing Dynasty Post Office. The highlight for kids is a traditional gondola ride through the canals — a truly magical experience!

Afternoon: Enjoy a farewell lunch at a local restaurant (included), then your guide will escort you to the airport or train station for your departure.
