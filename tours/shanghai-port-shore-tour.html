Tour Overview

Designed specifically for cruise passengers docking at Shanghai International Port, this shore excursion gives you a full-day city tour with seamless port pickup and drop-off. You'll visit Shanghai's must-see attractions — from the sky-high observation deck of Shanghai Tower (118th floor) to the classical beauty of Yu Garden and the lively Yuyuan Bazaar.

Explore the stylish Xintiandi district, shop along Nanjing Road, and end at the iconic Bund. The itinerary is flexible to accommodate your ship's docking schedule, and your guide will ensure you return to the port with plenty of time to spare.

Tour Highlights

🏗️

Shanghai Tower 118th Floor

Ride the world's fastest elevator to the top of China's tallest building for panoramic views.

🏯

Yu Garden

Explore this 400-year-old classical garden with exquisite pavilions, rockeries, and koi ponds.

⛩️

City God Temple

Visit the City God Temple, a vibrant Taoist temple complex. (Alternative when Yu Garden is closed on Monday.)

🏘️

Yuyuan Bazaar

Wander through the bustling market streets surrounding Yu Garden, perfect for souvenirs and local snacks.

🌆

Xintiandi

Stroll through this trendy Shikumen district with chic boutiques, cafes, and restored lane houses.

🌉

The Bund

Walk the famous waterfront promenade and take in the stunning Pudong skyline across the Huangpu River.

Full Itinerary

8:30 AM

Port Pickup

Your guide meets you at the cruise terminal at Shanghai International Port with a sign. Your vehicle is ready and waiting.

9:30 AM

Shanghai Tower

Ride the world's fastest elevator (73 km/h) to the 118th floor observation deck. Enjoy a 360-degree view of Shanghai's sprawling cityscape.

11:30 AM

Yu Garden or City God Temple

Visit the serene Yu Garden (closed Mondays — alternative: City God Temple). Then explore the bustling Yuyuan Bazaar and sample local snacks.

1:00 PM

Local Lunch

Enjoy a delicious lunch at a local restaurant near the Old City area.

2:30 PM

Xintiandi & Nanjing Road

Explore the stylish Xintiandi district, then walk along Nanjing Road, one of the world's busiest shopping streets.

4:00 PM

The Bund & Return to Port

Take in the iconic Bund skyline before your guide escorts you back to the cruise port with ample time before departure.
