5-Day Shanghai, Suzhou & Tongli Treasures Tour

5 Days / 4 Nights 4.8 (130+ reviews) Private Tour English Guide

Book This Tour

Tour Overview

Explore the treasures of the Yangtze River Delta on this 5-day journey through three of China's most culturally rich destinations. Start in Shanghai with its glittering skyscrapers, then travel by high-speed train to Suzhou, the 'Venice of the East,' famous for its UNESCO-listed classical gardens. End with a visit to Tongli, one of China's best-preserved water towns.

Tour Highlights

🏙️

Shanghai City Highlights

Explore the Bund, Yu Garden, Shanghai Tower, and the French Concession.

🏯

Humble Administrator's Garden

Suzhou's largest and most famous classical garden, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

🛶

Tongli Water Town

A beautifully preserved water town with canals, bridges, and Ming Dynasty residences.

🏛️

Suzhou Silk Museum

Learn about the 5,000-year history of silk production.

🚄

High-Speed Train

Travel from Shanghai to Suzhou in just 30 minutes.

🍜

Local Cuisine

Taste Suzhou's famous squirrel-shaped mandarin fish and Shanghai's xiaolongbao.

Full Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Shanghai

Welcome to Shanghai! Your guide and driver will meet you at the airport.

Day 2

Shanghai Full-Day Tour

Morning: Visit Yu Garden and the Yuyuan Bazaar.

Afternoon: Walk along The Bund. Ascend Shanghai Tower. Explore the French Concession.

Day 3

High-Speed Train to Suzhou

Morning: Ride the 30-minute high-speed train to Suzhou. Visit the Humble Administrator's Garden.

Afternoon: Explore Pingjiang Road and the Suzhou Silk Museum.

Day 4

Tongli Water Town Day Trip

Full Day at Tongli: Explore the Retreat & Reflection Garden (UNESCO), cross the Taiping Bridge, and take a gondola ride through the canals.

Day 5

Suzhou → Departure

Morning: Visit Tiger Hill and Shantang Street.

Afternoon: Transfer to Suzhou Station or Shanghai airports for departure.

Bullet Train

Water Towns

Silk
