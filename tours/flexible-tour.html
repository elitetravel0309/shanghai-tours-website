Tour Overview

This is the ultimate flexible tour — you decide what you want to see, how long you want to spend, and what pace you prefer. Your private guide will work with you to create a personalized itinerary that matches your interests perfectly.

Whether you want to explore Shanghai's hidden alleyways, dive into its food scene, visit museums and temples, or simply wander through the city with an expert local guide, this tour is designed to be whatever you want it to be. It's the perfect choice for return visitors, special interest travelers, or anyone who prefers a fully personalized experience.

How It Works

Step 1

Tell Us Your Interests

When you book, let us know what you're interested in — history, food, photography, shopping, architecture, or a mix of everything. Also tell us your available time and any special requests.

Step 2

We Design Your Itinerary

Your guide will create a personalized itinerary based on your preferences. You'll receive a proposal before the tour and can make any changes you'd like.

Step 3

Enjoy Your Custom Tour

Your private guide will meet you at your hotel and take you on a fully personalized Shanghai experience. The itinerary remains flexible — you can adjust on the fly!

Step 4

Ongoing Flexibility

Changed your mind mid-tour? No problem! Your guide is totally flexible and can adjust the route based on your mood, energy level, or discoveries along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is this Shanghai tour?

this tour covers Shanghai's highlights at a comfortable pace. The exact itinerary is flexible, and your private guide will tailor it to your interests and energy level.

Is this a private tour?

Yes, all our tours are private. You travel with your own group and a dedicated English-speaking guide — never shared with other travellers.

Can I customize the itinerary?

Absolutely. Every itinerary is fully customizable. Tell us what you most want to see, and we will adjust the tour to your preferences.

Does the price include hotel pickup?

For hotels in central Shanghai, door-to-door pickup and drop-off are typically included. Share your hotel details and we will confirm the exact arrangement.

How do I book and pay?

Booking is easy — message us on WhatsApp or email. For most tours no deposit is required and you can pay on the day, with no hidden fees.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. We offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before your scheduled tour, and date or itinerary changes are always welcome.
