Tour Overview

This all-inclusive private VIP tour is the perfect day for couples, families and small groups that want a truly special experience of both modern and historic Shanghai. You'll enjoy a wide range of sites and activities along with an evening river cruise, fantastic aerial views from Shanghai Tower, and a spectacular Sichuan dinner with a unique face-changing opera show.

This luxury tour comes complete with a premium vehicle and driver, authentic lunch, tea ceremony, and VIP seats on the Huangpu River Cruise. Every detail is taken care of so you can focus on creating unforgettable memories.

🏯

Explore a 400-year-old classical Ming Dynasty garden in the heart of Shanghai's old city.

📚

Visit the 1000-year-old temple and enjoy a peaceful traditional tea ceremony.

Full Itinerary

10:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide and driver will pick up your party at your centrally located hotel to start this 11-hour all-inclusive VIP tour.

10:30 AM

Yu Garden & Surrounding Area

Your first stop is Yu Garden, the best place to understand and enjoy Chinese culture and history. The surrounding area is also a great shopping kingdom to experience real Chinese folk culture, especially during festival periods!

12:00 PM

Confucius Temple & Tea Ceremony

Visit the Confucius Temple, over 1000 years old, the place to remember the great thinker and philosopher Confucius. This beautiful area and garden is perfect for photos. You'll also enjoy a very peaceful and informative traditional tea ceremony here.

1:00 PM

Authentic Local Lunch

An authentic lunch will be served in a typical Chinese environment. Your meal with rich flavors is guaranteed to satisfy your appetite.

2:30 PM

Tianzifang & Shanghai Tower

Visit Tianzifang, a fantastic leisure area combining commercial and local daily life with private artists' studios and street bars. Then head to the Shanghai Tower — the highest building in China — for an amazing panoramic view of the city.

5:00 PM

The Bund & Huangpu River Cruise (VIP)

Head to the famous Bund for a 1-hour river cruise along the Huangpu River with VIP seats. See amazing views on both the modern Pudong side and the historic west side of Shanghai. Depending on the time of year, you'll also see dazzling lights unfold.

6:45 PM

Sichuan Dinner & Face-Changing Opera Show

Enjoy a delicious Sichuan cuisine dinner while watching a classic face-changing opera show (45 minutes). This is a mesmerizing performance where performers change masks in the blink of an eye — a true Chinese cultural treasure.

9:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

After the show, you'll be transferred back to your hotel to enjoy the rest of your evening.
