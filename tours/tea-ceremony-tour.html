Tour Overview

Immerse yourself in Chinese tea culture on this specialized tour that explores the fascinating world of Chinese tea, from its ancient origins to its modern appreciation. Visit Shanghai's most famous tea markets, traditional tea houses, and participate in an authentic tea ceremony experience.

Your tea culture specialist guide will share the history, philosophy, and art of Chinese tea, providing insights into the different varieties, brewing techniques, and cultural significance of tea in Chinese society.

Tour Highlights

🍵

Tianshan Tea Market

Explore Shanghai's largest tea market, where hundreds of vendors offer teas from all regions of China, along with teaware, accessories, and tea-related artifacts.

🏯

Yu Garden Tea Houses

Visit traditional tea houses in the historic Yu Garden area, where you can sample teas in a classical Chinese garden setting surrounded by pavilions and rockeries.

🫖

Traditional Tea Ceremony

Participate in an authentic Chinese tea ceremony (Gongfu Cha), learning the precise movements, timing, and philosophy behind this ancient art form.

🌱

Tea Varieties Education

Learn about the six main types of Chinese tea — green, white, yellow, oolong, black, and pu-erh — their origins, processing methods, and unique characteristics.

🛒

Tea Purchasing Guidance

Receive expert advice on selecting and purchasing high-quality teas directly from market vendors, with tips on storage, preparation, and appreciation.

🧘

Mindfulness & Philosophy

Discover the philosophical aspects of tea culture, including mindfulness, harmony, and respect, which have influenced Chinese art, poetry, and daily life for centuries.

Full Itinerary

9:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your tea culture specialist guide will meet you in your hotel lobby. After a brief introduction to Chinese tea culture, you'll head to your first destination in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

10:00 AM

Tianshan Tea Market Exploration

Begin at Shanghai's largest tea market. Wander through the aisles of vendors, sampling different teas, learning about their origins, and observing the traditional methods of tea preparation and storage.

11:30 AM

Tea Tasting Session

Participate in a guided tea tasting session at the market, sampling several varieties of tea while your guide explains the characteristics, brewing techniques, and health benefits of each type.

12:30 PM

Lunch at Tea-Themed Restaurant

Enjoy lunch at a restaurant featuring tea-infused dishes and traditional Chinese cuisine, experiencing how tea influences culinary traditions.

2:00 PM

Yu Garden Tea House Visit

Visit a traditional tea house in the historic Yu Garden area. Experience tea service in a classical Chinese garden setting, surrounded by pavilions, rockeries, and traditional architecture.

3:00 PM

Traditional Tea Ceremony Experience

Participate in an authentic Chinese tea ceremony (Gongfu Cha). Learn the precise movements, timing, and philosophy behind this ancient art form, and appreciate the mindfulness it cultivates.

4:30 PM

Tea Shopping & Recommendations

Receive expert guidance on selecting and purchasing high-quality teas. Your guide will recommend reputable vendors and help you choose teas to take home as souvenirs or gifts.

5:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide will escort you back to your hotel, leaving you with new tea knowledge, perhaps some purchased teas, and a deeper appreciation for Chinese tea culture.

Important Information

Walking Level

Low to moderate. Comfortable shoes recommended. Approximately 5,000-7,000 steps with plenty of sitting during tea experiences.

Tea Consumption

Multiple tea tastings throughout the day. Caffeine-sensitive individuals should inform guide in advance for alternative arrangements.

Family Friendly

Suitable for all ages. Children often enjoy the tea tasting experience. Non-caffeinated options available for kids.

Weather

Operates in all weather conditions. Indoor tea houses and markets provide shelter. Umbrellas provided if needed.

Purchasing Opportunities

Bring a bag or backpack for any tea purchases. Guide helps with selection, packaging, and shipping options for large quantities.
