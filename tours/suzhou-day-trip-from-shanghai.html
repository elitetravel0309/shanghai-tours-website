Tour Overview

Hop aboard a high-speed train and travel 30 minutes from Shanghai to Suzhou, the "Venice of the East." Known for its classical gardens, ancient canals, and silk heritage, Suzhou is a UNESCO World Heritage gem and a must-visit destination for anyone wanting to see China's classical beauty.

Your day includes a visit to the UNESCO-listed Humble Administrator's Garden, a stroll along historic Pingjiang Road, a visit to the Suzhou Museum (designed by I.M. Pei), and a relaxing boat ride along the Grand Canal. With a private guide and all logistics handled, this is a stress-free way to explore one of China's most beautiful cities.

Tour Highlights

🌿

Humble Administrator's Garden

Explore China's most famous classical garden, a UNESCO World Heritage site with stunning pavilions, ponds, and rockeries.

🏘️

Pingjiang Road

Stroll along this historic canal-side street lined with traditional shops, teahouses, and local artisans.

🏛️

Suzhou Museum

Admire the masterful architecture by I.M. Pei and explore the museum's collection of ancient Chinese art and artifacts.

⛵

Grand Canal Boat Ride

Cruise along the Grand Canal, the world's longest canal, and see Suzhou from a unique water-level perspective.

🧣

Silk Museum

Discover Suzhou's silk heritage, from silkworm to loom, at the fascinating Suzhou Silk Museum.

🍜

Local Lunch

Enjoy authentic Suzhou cuisine, known for its delicate flavors and seasonal ingredients.

Full Itinerary

8:00 AM

Hotel Pickup & High-Speed Train

Your guide picks you up and escorts you to the train station. Board the high-speed train to Suzhou — just 30 minutes away!

9:00 AM

Humble Administrator's Garden

Explore Suzhou's most famous classical garden. Wander through exquisite pavilions, lotus ponds, and winding rockeries that have inspired poets for centuries.

11:30 AM

Local Lunch & Pingjiang Road

Enjoy a traditional Suzhou lunch, then stroll along the historic Pingjiang Road, a charming canal-side street with artisan shops and tea houses.

1:30 PM

Suzhou Museum or Silk Museum

Visit the Suzhou Museum, designed by world-renowned architect I.M. Pei, or opt for the Silk Museum to learn about Suzhou's silk-making heritage.

3:00 PM

Grand Canal Boat Ride

Enjoy a serene boat ride along the Grand Canal, the world's longest man-made waterway, and see Suzhou's canal-side architecture from the water.

4:30 PM

Return to Shanghai

Your guide escorts you back to the train station for the high-speed return to Shanghai, where your driver will be waiting to take you back to your hotel.
