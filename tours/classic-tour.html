Tour Overview

Experience the very best of Shanghai in one unforgettable day! This comprehensive full-day tour takes you through the city's most iconic landmarks, from the colonial-era Bund to the futuristic Shanghai Tower, with a local expert guide who will bring the city's fascinating stories to life.

Perfect for first-time visitors, this tour covers all the must-see attractions while also revealing hidden gems that most tourists miss. Your private guide will tailor the pace and focus to your interests, ensuring a truly personalized experience.

Tour Highlights

🏛️

The Bund

Stroll along Shanghai's famous waterfront, admiring the magnificent colonial architecture that earned the city the nickname "Paris of the East."

🏯

Yu Garden & Old City

Wander through a classical Ming Dynasty garden with exquisite pavilions, rockeries, and ponds, then explore the bustling Old City bazaar.

🌆

Shanghai Tower

Soar to the 118th floor of China's tallest building for breathtaking 360-degree views of the entire city from the highest observation deck in the world.

🌳

French Concession

Discover the leafy, art-deco streets of Shanghai's most charming neighborhood, home to boutique shops, café terraces, and plane-tree-lined avenues.

🚢

Huangpu River Cruise

End your day with a relaxing cruise along the Huangpu River, watching the Pudong skyline light up as the sun sets over the Bund.

🍜

Local Lunch Experience

Enjoy an authentic Shanghainese lunch at a locally-loved restaurant, featuring signature dishes like xiaolongbao, braised pork, and seasonal vegetables.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide will meet you in your hotel lobby. After a brief introduction, you'll head to your first destination in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

9:30 AM

The Bund Walk

Begin at the Bund, Shanghai's iconic waterfront. Your guide will explain the history behind the grand buildings — from the Customs House to the Peace Hotel — and point out the best photo spots with the Pudong skyline as your backdrop.

10:30 AM

Yu Garden & Old City Bazaar

Enter the tranquil Yu Garden, a masterpiece of Ming Dynasty landscape design. Afterwards, explore the lively Old City Bazaar where you can shop for souvenirs, watch artisans at work, and taste local snacks.

12:30 PM

Shanghainese Lunch

Enjoy a delicious lunch at a carefully selected local restaurant. Your guide will help you order the best dishes and explain the culinary traditions behind each one.

2:00 PM

Shanghai Tower Observation Deck

Take the high-speed elevator (the fastest in the world!) to the 118th floor of the Shanghai Tower. Spend time taking in the panoramic views and walking on the glass floor if you dare!

3:30 PM

French Concession Stroll

Explore the former French Concession, one of Shanghai's most atmospheric neighborhoods. Walk along Wukang Road and Anfu Road, lined with historic villas, trendy boutiques, and sidewalk cafés.

5:00 PM

Huangpu River Sunset Cruise

Board a cruise boat on the Huangpu River. As you sail between the Bund and Pudong, watch the city transition from day to night — the buildings light up and the skyline becomes a dazzling display.

6:30 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide will escort you back to your hotel, leaving you with a full memory card and a deeper appreciation for Shanghai's incredible story.
