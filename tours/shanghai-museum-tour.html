Tour Overview

Dive into Shanghai's rich and layered history through its most fascinating museums. This tour takes you from the futuristic Urban Planning Exhibition Center — where a breathtaking 5D digital sandbox brings the city's future to life — to the intimate and eye-opening Shanghai Propaganda Poster Art Center, home to over 6,000 posters spanning 1913 to 1997.

Between museum visits, you'll explore the stylish Xintiandi district, a restored Shikumen neighborhood blending old-world charm with modern boutiques, and wander through Tianzifang, a labyrinth of narrow alleyways filled with art studios, craft shops, and hidden cafes. It's a perfect day for history buffs, art lovers, and curious travelers alike.

Full Itinerary

9:00 AM

Hotel Pickup

Your guide meets you at your hotel for a day of museum exploration.

9:30 AM

Urban Planning Exhibition Center

Explore Shanghai's ambitious urban blueprint. Highlights include the massive city model and the immersive 5D digital sandbox. (Closed Wednesdays — alternative: Shanghai Museum.)

12:00 PM

Lunch at Local Restaurant

Enjoy a delicious local lunch at a restaurant near the museum district.

1:30 PM

Propaganda Poster Art Center

Discover one of the world's most comprehensive collections of Chinese propaganda posters, spanning eight decades of history. (Closed Mondays.)

3:00 PM

Xintiandi & Tianzifang

Walk through the trendy Xintiandi district, then lose yourself in the artistic alleyways of Tianzifang. Browse galleries, pick up unique souvenirs, and enjoy a coffee in a hidden courtyard.

5:00 PM

Hotel Drop-off

Your guide escorts you back to your hotel, full of stories and insights about Shanghai's rich cultural tapestry.
