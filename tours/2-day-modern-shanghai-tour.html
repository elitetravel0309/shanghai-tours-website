Tour Overview

Discover the cutting-edge side of Shanghai on this 2-day modern impression tour. From the futuristic skyline of Lujiazui to the creative energy of the M50 Art District, and from the world's largest Starbucks Reserve Roastery to the trendy boutiques of Xintiandi — this tour showcases Shanghai as a global hub of innovation, style, and contemporary culture.

Perfect for travelers interested in architecture, art, design, and the pulse of modern Chinese urban life. Your private guide will share insights into Shanghai's transformation from a traditional port city to one of the world's most dynamic metropolises.

Full Itinerary

Day 1

Modern Shanghai: Skyline & Art

Morning: Your guide and driver will pick you up from your hotel. Start in the Lujiazui Financial District in Pudong, home to Shanghai's iconic skyline. Ascend the Shanghai Tower (632m) — China's tallest building — via the world's fastest elevator to the 118th-floor observation deck.

Midday: Visit the Starbucks Reserve Roastery on Nanjing Road — the world's largest Starbucks at 30,000 square feet across two floors. Experience artisan coffee roasting, sip on exclusive reserve blends, and enjoy the stunning interior design.

Afternoon: Explore M50 Art District on Moganshan Road, housed in a former textile mill. This creative hub features dozens of contemporary art galleries, studios, and avant-garde exhibitions.

Evening: Enjoy dinner in the French Concession area, then stroll along The Bund to see the spectacular nighttime illumination of the Pudong skyline.

Day 2

Lifestyle & Culture: Xintiandi to Maglev

Morning: Visit Xintiandi, a vibrant pedestrian district where traditional Shikumen (stone-gate) houses have been transformed into trendy boutiques, cafes, and restaurants. Explore the Shikumen Museum to learn about Shanghai's 1920s-30s middle-class lifestyle.

Midday: Wander through the Former French Concession, with its tree-lined avenues, Art Deco villas, and hidden garden bars. Drive along Huaihai Road and Wukang Road — two of Shanghai's most beautiful streets.

Afternoon: Experience the legendary Shanghai Maglev Train! Ride the world's first commercial high-speed maglev line from Longyang Road to Pudong Airport, reaching 431 km/h in just 7 minutes. Your guide will escort you to the airport or train station for departure.
