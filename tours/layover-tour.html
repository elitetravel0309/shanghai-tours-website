Tour Overview

Turn your airport layover into an unforgettable Shanghai adventure! Whether you have 4 hours or 8 hours between flights, this flexible tour is designed around your schedule. Your private guide will meet you at the airport, take you to the city's top attractions, and ensure you're back in plenty of time for your next flight.

This tour is fully customizable — you decide how long you want to explore and what you want to see. From the iconic Bund and Yu Garden to the Shanghai Tower or a quick food tour, your guide will create the perfect itinerary based on your available time and interests.

Making the most of a layover in Shanghai has never been easier. This flexible tour is specially designed for transit passengers with limited time. Whether you have 4, 6, or 8 hours between flights, we'll create a customized itinerary that shows you Shanghai's highlights without the stress of navigating on your own.

Your guide will meet you at the airport or Hongqiao Railway Station, handle all logistics, and ensure you're back in time for your onward journey. Choose from a range of options — from a quick Bund visit to a full highlights tour including Yu Garden, Shanghai Tower, and a local lunch. The route is flexible and can be adjusted based on your arrival time, traffic conditions, and personal interests. Includes airport baggage storage coordination.

Tour Highlights

✈️

Airport Meet & Greet

Your guide meets you at the airport with a sign, handles your luggage, and whisks you directly into the city — no waiting, no navigation.

🏛️

The Bund Express

Visit the iconic Bund waterfront, perfect for a quick photo stop with the famous Pudong skyline backdrop.

Yu Garden Quick Visit

Shanghai Tower (Optional)

If time permits, ascend to the top of China's tallest building for a breathtaking 360-degree view of the city.

🍜

Local Lunch Experience

Enjoy a quick but authentic Shanghai meal at a carefully selected local restaurant — from soup dumplings to hand-pulled noodles.

🛍️

Nanjing Road Walk

Take a quick stroll down China's premier shopping street, perfect for last-minute souvenirs and photos.

Full Itinerary

4-Hour

Bund & Lunch Express

Perfect for 6-7 hour layovers (1 hour each way from airport). Meet your guide, drive directly to the Bund for photos and a short walk, enjoy a quick xiaolongbao lunch at a nearby restaurant, stop at Nanjing Road for a quick shop, and return to the airport with plenty of time to spare.

6-Hour

Bund, Yu Garden & Lunch

For 8-9 hour layovers. Visit the Bund for photos, explore Yu Garden and the Old City Bazaar, enjoy a sit-down Shanghai lunch, walk along Nanjing Road, and return to the airport. This option gives you a well-rounded taste of Shanghai's highlights.

8-Hour

Full Shanghai Highlights

For 10+ hour layovers. The full experience: Bund walk, Yu Garden visit, Shanghai Tower observation deck, local lunch, French Concession drive-through, and Nanjing Road shopping. Essentially a full-day Shanghai tour compressed into a layover-friendly format.

Custom

Flexible Itinerary

Have a specific interest? Whether it's Shanghai's art scene, tea culture, or a particular neighborhood, we can customize the layover tour to match your interests and available time. Just let us know your preferences when booking.

Important Information

Minimum 4 Hours

You need at least 4 hours between flights. For a 4-hour option, we focus on the Bund and Yu Garden area.

Both Airports

We serve both Pudong (PVG) and Hongqiao (SHA) airports. Pickup and drop-off at the same airport are included.

Visa Requirements

Shanghai offers 144-hour visa-free transit for many nationalities. Check with your airline or embassy before booking.
