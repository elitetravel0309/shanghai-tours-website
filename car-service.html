Must-See Shanghai Attractions

The Bund (Waitan)

Shanghai's iconic waterfront promenade with colonial-era buildings opposite the Pudong skyline. Best at sunset. Free admission.

Yu Garden (Yuyuan)

Classical Ming Dynasty garden with rockeries, pavilions, and koi ponds. The bazaar offers xiaolongbao. ¥40 admission.

Shanghai Tower

World's second-tallest building at 632m. 118th-floor observation deck with 360° views. ¥180 admission.

French Concession

Tree-lined streets, Art Deco architecture, trendy cafes. Xintiandi and Tianzifang for shopping. Free admission.

Nanjing Road

One of the world's busiest shopping streets, 5.5km from the Bund to Jing'an Temple. Free admission.

Shanghai Disneyland

Largest Disney castle in the world. TRON Lightcycle, Pirates of the Caribbean. ¥475-¥719.
