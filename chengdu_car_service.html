Must-See Chengdu Attractions

Giant Panda Base

Home to over 200 giant pandas. Visit early morning for best panda activity. ¥55 admission.

Leshan Giant Buddha

World's largest stone Buddha at 71m tall, carved in cliff face. Take a boat ride for full view. ¥80 admission.

Jinli Ancient Street

Historic street with traditional Sichuan architecture, snacks, and handicrafts. Best at night with lanterns. Free admission.

Wuhou Shrine

Dedicated to Zhuge Liang, the legendary strategist. Beautiful gardens and historical exhibits. ¥50 admission.

Kuanzhai Alley

Three parallel alleys with teahouses, local snacks, and cultural performances. Great for people watching. Free admission.

Dujiangyan

2,200-year-old irrigation system still in use. Engineering marvel and UNESCO site. ¥80 admission.
