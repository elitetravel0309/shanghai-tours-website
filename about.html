Our Story

We've Been Showing Travelers the Real Shanghai Since 2010

What started as a small group of passionate local guides has grown into Shanghai's most trusted private tour operator. We believe that travel is about connection — connecting with people, culture, and the authentic spirit of a place.

Our team is made up of Shanghai natives who know every hidden alley, every family-run restaurant, and every breathtaking viewpoint. We don't just show you the sights — we share our home with you.
